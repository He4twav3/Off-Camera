ONCAMERA - UGC CREATOR AGREEMENT (PER-POST PAY)
A template for brands working with creators through OnCamera.

This is a starting point, not legal advice. Fill in the [brackets], and have a
lawyer review it before you rely on it, especially for larger campaigns or paid
usage. Delete anything that does not apply.

------------------------------------------------------------
1. Parties
------------------------------------------------------------
Brand:    [Company name, address, contact email]
Creator:  [Full name, handle(s), contact email]
Campaign: [Campaign name]
Date:     [Date]

------------------------------------------------------------
2. What the creator makes
------------------------------------------------------------
- Short videos for: [product / app / service]
- Platforms: [TikTok / Instagram / YouTube Shorts]
- Brief: [link to the campaign brief]
- Every video must be new and made for this campaign. No repeats, re-uploads,
  or bulk posting to fill the count.
- Music, images, fonts and footage must be ones the creator has the rights to.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. Posting and disclosure
------------------------------------------------------------
- Videos are posted on the creator's own accounts.
- Every post is labelled as a paid partnership, using the platform's label or
  #ad clearly at the start of the caption.
- Each post stays public for at least [90] days after it goes live.

------------------------------------------------------------
4. Pay
------------------------------------------------------------
- Base pay: [$20] per approved post.
- View bonus, per post, at the highest milestone it reaches in its counting
  window. Bonuses do not stack: a post that reaches 5K after 1K earns the 5K
  bonus in total.
    [1,000 views: $2]   [5,000 views: $10]
    [10,000 views: $20] [100,000 views: $200]
- Counting window: only views within [30] days of a post going live count.
  After that the number is final.
- Payment schedule: the base pay is paid after every [15] posts the creator
  delivers. Bonuses are paid once a post's counting window has ended.
- Views are counted from the platform and refreshed about once a day. The
  count shown in OnCamera is the one used for payment.
- The brand pays the creator directly, in [US dollars], within [7] days of
  each statement, and covers any transfer fees so the creator receives the
  full amount.

------------------------------------------------------------
5. Rights to use the videos
------------------------------------------------------------
- The creator owns the videos. The creator gives the brand a licence to:
    [ ] repost organically on the brand's own channels
    [ ] run the videos as paid ads / whitelisting (platforms: [ ])
- Licence length: [e.g. 12 months from the date of each post]
- Territory: [worldwide / list]
- Anything beyond this needs a separate written agreement and fee.
- The brand does not edit the videos in a way that misrepresents the creator.

------------------------------------------------------------
6. Product and claims
------------------------------------------------------------
- The brand gives the creator accurate information about the product and any
  claims they may make, and does not ask for claims that are false or that
  need approval the brand does not have.
- The creator gives honest opinions and does not make claims the brand has not
  approved.

------------------------------------------------------------
7. Ending the agreement
------------------------------------------------------------
- Either side can stop with [7] days written notice (email is fine).
- Posts already delivered and approved are still paid in full. Posts still in
  their counting window keep counting until it ends.
- A post may be removed, and not paid, if it breaks section 2 or 3.

------------------------------------------------------------
8. General
------------------------------------------------------------
- The creator works as an independent contractor, not an employee.
- Each side keeps the other's private information private.
- This agreement is the whole deal between the two sides. Changes must be in
  writing (email is fine).
- Governing law: [country / state].
