OFF CAMERA — COLD PITCH EMAIL TEMPLATES
From Module 5: Pitching Brands & Landing Your First Deal

------------------------------------------------------------
TEMPLATE 1 — The cold pitch
------------------------------------------------------------
Subject: Content idea for [Brand] (faceless UGC)

Hi [Name],

I make faceless UGC for [niche/category] brands, most recently a
[format, e.g. "3-video unboxing series"] for [comparable brand or
"a similar brand in your space"].

I had an idea for [Brand] specifically: [one-line concept tied to a
real product or campaign they're currently running].

Here's an example of similar work: [link to your best piece]

If that's useful, I'd love to put together a short package for you.
Rates and turnaround below if you want them up front:
[link to your rate card, or 2-3 lines with price + turnaround]

Best,
[Your name]

------------------------------------------------------------
TEMPLATE 2 — The follow-up (send 4-5 days later, no reply)
------------------------------------------------------------
Subject: Re: Content idea for [Brand]

Hi [Name], just floating this back up in case it got buried.

Still happy to put a sample together this week if it's useful, no
pressure either way.

[Your name]

------------------------------------------------------------
TEMPLATE 3 — Replying to an inbound offer
------------------------------------------------------------
Subject: Re: [their subject line]

Hi [Name],

Thanks for reaching out, I'd love to. A couple quick questions so I
can send an accurate quote:

- How many videos, and what length/format?
- Usage rights: organic only, or paid social too, and for how long?
- Rough timeline for delivery?

Once I have those I'll send a rate and turnaround.

[Your name]

------------------------------------------------------------
Notes
------------------------------------------------------------
- Keep the first email under 120 words. Busy inboxes skim.
- Always attach or link an example, never describe your work only in text.
- Price on request beats a number in the first email, it starts a
  conversation instead of ending one.
