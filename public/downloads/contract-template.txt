OFF CAMERA — UGC DELIVERABLE CONTRACT (SHORT FORM)
From Module 8: Pricing, Contracts & Getting Paid

Use this for small, single-deliverable UGC jobs. For larger retainers,
have a lawyer adapt it, this is a starting point, not legal advice.

------------------------------------------------------------
1. Parties
------------------------------------------------------------
Creator: [Your name / business name]
Brand:   [Brand / company name]
Date:    [Date]

------------------------------------------------------------
2. Deliverables
------------------------------------------------------------
- Quantity: [e.g. "3 vertical UGC videos, 15-30 seconds each"]
- Format: [aspect ratio, raw + edited, captions on/off, etc.]
- Brief/reference: [link or attached brief]
- Revisions included: [e.g. "1 round of revisions per video"]

------------------------------------------------------------
3. Usage rights
------------------------------------------------------------
- Organic (brand's own social channels): [Yes/No, duration]
- Paid social / whitelisting: [Yes/No, duration, platforms]
- Anything beyond this scope requires a separate usage fee.

------------------------------------------------------------
4. Timeline
------------------------------------------------------------
- Raw footage / draft delivered by: [date]
- Final delivery by: [date]
- Brand feedback window: [e.g. "2 business days per round"]

------------------------------------------------------------
5. Payment
------------------------------------------------------------
- Total fee: [amount]
- Terms: [e.g. "50% on booking, 50% on delivery" or "net-7 on delivery"]
- Late payment: [e.g. "invoices unpaid after 14 days accrue 1.5%/month"]

------------------------------------------------------------
6. Cancellation
------------------------------------------------------------
- If cancelled before filming: [e.g. "deposit is non-refundable"]
- If cancelled after delivery of raw footage: [full fee due]

------------------------------------------------------------
