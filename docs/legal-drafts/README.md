# Legal drafts: for a lawyer to review

**These are drafts, not legal advice, and nothing in this folder is live on the site.**
They are written from what the OnCamera codebase actually does today, so a
lawyer can start from the real facts instead of from a blank page.

Every `[BRACKET]` is a decision or fact only you can supply. Items marked
**LAWYER:** are points where the right answer depends on where you operate.

## Files
- `terms-of-service.md`: replaces the course-era Terms page.
- `privacy-policy.md`: replaces the course-era Privacy page.
- `creator-agreement.md`: what each creator accepts before taking a campaign.
- `brand-agreement.md`: what each brand accepts before a campaign starts.

## Decisions these drafts assume (change them if they are wrong)
1. Brands pay the creator's rate to OnCamera; OnCamera keeps a commission taken
   **from the creator's share** (option "a"), so brands pay no agency fee.
2. Creators are **independent contractors**, not employees.
3. Creators are paid **after** the brand has paid and the post is approved.
4. Every paid post must carry a paid-partnership label or #ad.
5. Creators are 18 or over.

## Questions a lawyer should answer
- Does taking a commission from creators make OnCamera a licensed "talent
  agency" or "employment agency" where you operate or where creators live?
- Holding brand money before paying creators: do you need a payments licence,
  or must you use a licensed payment provider to hold and split funds?
- Which tax forms and VAT / sales tax apply to brand invoices and creator
  payouts?
- Which company entity and governing law should appear in each document?
- GDPR / UK GDPR: is a data-processing agreement needed with Supabase, Vercel,
  Resend, Apify and the payment provider, and are international transfers
  covered?
