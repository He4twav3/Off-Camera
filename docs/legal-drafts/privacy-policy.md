## What we collect
**When you create an account:** your name, email address and password (stored hashed by our authentication provider), and whether you are a creator or a brand.
**Creators, in the profile:** date of birth, country and city, username, social handles and follower counts, content types, skills, languages, experience, brands you've worked with, bio, portfolio link, rates and availability.
**Brands:** company name, website, contact name.
**Using the service:** the posts you submit, view counts for them, campaign status, payout amounts and payment status, and messages we send you.
**Technical data:** your IP address and device/browser details (used for security and to limit abuse), and a session cookie that keeps you signed in.
**Signups on public pages:** the details you submit on a campaign signup form.
**Later, when payouts are live:** payment details and tax information needed to pay you. **LAWYER:** update this when a payment provider is chosen.

## Why we use it
- To run your account and the campaigns you take part in (contract).
- To verify that your social accounts are yours and to count the views of your posts (contract / legitimate interest).
- To pay you or invoice you, and keep financial records (contract / legal obligation).
- To keep the service safe and prevent fraud and abuse (legitimate interest).
- To email you about your account and, if you opt in, campaign updates (contract / consent).
